## `01. About`

I am pursuing a **B.Tech + M.Tech Dual Degree in Computer Science** at  
**The LNM Institute of Information Technology, Jaipur** — Class of 2028.

My work lives at the intersection of three questions:

- How can AI make complicated information easier to understand?
- How do we turn an experimental model into a dependable product?
- What separates an impressive prototype from software people actually use?

I enjoy building **GenAI applications, full-stack products, intelligent interfaces, and data-driven systems**. I am especially interested in applied NLP, LLM APIs, quantitative research, explainability, and product engineering.

> I do not want AI to merely generate more information.  
> I want it to make the next decision clearer.
